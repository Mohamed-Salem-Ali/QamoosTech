# AI & Data Concepts

### OCR (Optical Character Recognition)
- **Definition**: The electronic conversion of images of typed, handwritten, or printed text into machine-encoded text.
- **Context/Stack**: Computer Vision / Document Processing
- **Usage Examples**:
  - *Technical*: "We use Tesseract OCR to extract the raw text from scanned invoices."
  - *Conversational*: "The OCR pipeline is failing on blurry images, we need better preprocessing."
- **Related Terms**: Image Processing, Tesseract

### LLM (Large Language Model)
- **Definition**: A computational model capable of generating language and other types of content to achieve a wide range of tasks.
- **Context/Stack**: AI / Natural Language Processing
- **Usage Examples**:
  - *Technical*: "The parsed OCR text is fed into a Gemini LLM to extract structured JSON data."
  - *Conversational*: "Can we prompt the LLM to summarize the extracted text?"
- **Related Terms**: Generative AI, Prompt Engineering
