# Freelancing

---

### Deliverable
- **Pronunciation**: "dih-LIV-er-uh-bul" · ديليفَرابل
- **Arabic**: المُخْرَجات / ما سيتم تسليمه
- **Definition**: A tangible or intangible product or service provided to a client upon the completion of a project phase (e.g., a Dockerized backend, a UI mockup, a deployed database).
- **Context**: Project proposals, milestone planning, invoicing.
- **Usage Examples**:
  - *Formal*: "The final deliverable for Phase 1 is a fully containerized NestJS API deployed to an AWS EC2 instance."
  - *Casual*: "What exactly are the deliverables for this week? Just the database schema, or the endpoints too?"
- **Common Mistake**: Using vague deliverables like "do the backend." Always specify exactly what will be handed over (code, documentation, deployed app).
- **Related Terms**: Milestone, Scope, Handoff

### Turnaround Time (TAT)
- **Pronunciation**: "TURN-uh-round tym" · تيرن أراوند تايم
- **Arabic**: وقت الإنجاز / مدة التنفيذ
- **Definition**: The amount of time it takes to complete a process or fulfill a request, from the moment it is received to the moment the deliverable is handed over.
- **Context**: Client communication, sprint estimation, bug resolution.
- **Usage Examples**:
  - *Formal*: "For critical severity bugs, our SLA guarantees a turnaround time of under 24 hours."
  - *Casual*: "I can do this, but the turnaround time will be about a week since I'm booked up."
- **Common Mistake**: Overpromising a fast turnaround time without padding for unexpected blockers, leading to missed deadlines.
- **Related Terms**: ETA, Deadline, SLA (Service Level Agreement)
