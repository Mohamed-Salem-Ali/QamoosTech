# Agile & Scrum Terminology

### Scrum Ceremonies
- **Definition**: Structured meetings used by Agile teams (e.g., Daily Standup, Sprint Planning, Sprint Review, Sprint Retrospective).
- **Context/Stack**: Project Management / Team Collaboration
- **Usage Examples**:
  - *Technical/Workplace*: "We discuss blockers during our daily Scrum ceremonies."
  - *Conversational*: "Let's bring this up in the retrospective ceremony."
- **Related Terms**: Standup, Sprint, Agile

### Cross-functional Teams
- **Definition**: A group of people with different functional expertise working toward a common goal.
- **Context/Stack**: Team Dynamics / Organizational Structure
- **Usage Examples**:
  - *Technical/Workplace*: "I collaborated in a cross-functional team including frontend developers, ML engineers, and domain experts."
  - *Conversational*: "Working cross-functionally helped us solve the integration issue much faster."
- **Related Terms**: Collaboration, Interdisciplinary
