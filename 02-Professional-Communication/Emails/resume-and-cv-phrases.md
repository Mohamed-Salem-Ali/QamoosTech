# Resume & High-Impact Action Phrases

### "Owning the whole path to production"
- **Goal/Tone**: Demonstrating end-to-end responsibility and leadership.
- **Context**: Used in interviews or cover letters to show you don't just write code, but also deploy and maintain it.
- **Example**: "I am comfortable owning the whole path to production, from initial system design to configuring AWS infrastructure."

### "Bias toward [X] over [Y]"
- **Goal/Tone**: Clearly stating your engineering philosophy.
- **Context**: Explaining trade-offs in architecture or work style.
- **Example**: "I have a strong bias toward data security and testing discipline over shipping fast and hoping."

### "Shortening the feedback loop"
- **Goal/Tone**: Showing efficiency and focus on user/client satisfaction.
- **Context**: Agile methodologies and client communications.
- **Example**: "By rapidly building proof-of-concept demos, I shortened the feedback loop on feature decisions."

### "Event-driven pipelines" & "Immutable entries"
- **Goal/Tone**: Highlighting advanced architectural knowledge.
- **Example**: "Implemented a double-entry accounting engine with immutable journal entries and event-driven pipelines for async processing."
