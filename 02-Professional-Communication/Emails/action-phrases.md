# Action Phrases

---

### "Self-driven growth" / "Continuous Learning"
- **Pronunciation**: "self DRIV-en grohth" · سيلف دريفن جروث
- **Arabic**: النمو الذاتي / التعلم المستمر
- **Meaning**: Demonstrating that you proactively seek out new skills and knowledge without being told or required to.
- **Context**: Cover letters, LinkedIn "About" section, interview answers about personal development.
- **Usage Examples**:
  - *Formal*: "My self-driven growth in cloud architecture and AI has led me to acquire 10+ industry certifications, including three from Anthropic."
  - *Casual*: "I just like learning new things — I'm always picking up a new framework on the side."
- **Common Mistake**: Listing certificates without connecting them to real outcomes. "I got certified in AWS" is weak. "I got certified in AWS, then migrated our staging environment from a single EC2 instance to ECS Fargate" shows the learning had impact.
- **Key Vocabulary**: Initiative, proactive, upskilling, autodidact

### "Scalable"
- **Pronunciation**: "SKAY-luh-bul" · سكيلابل
- **Arabic**: قابل للتوسع
- **Meaning**: A system or design that can handle increasing load or complexity without requiring a fundamental redesign.
- **Context**: Architecture discussions, system design interviews, technical writing.
- **Usage Examples**:
  - *Formal*: "I designed scalable database schemas that accommodate high user growth across multiple domains without migration-breaking changes."
  - *Casual*: "Is this approach going to scale, or will we hit a wall at 10K users?"
- **Common Mistake**: Using "scalable" as a buzzword without substance. Don't say "I built a scalable API" — say *how* it scales: "horizontally via stateless containers behind a load balancer" or "vertically with read replicas for the reporting queries."
- **Key Vocabulary**: Horizontal scaling, vertical scaling, elasticity, bottleneck
