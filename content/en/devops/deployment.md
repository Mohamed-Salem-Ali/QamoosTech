## Definition

Putting a new version of the software on a server so real users can use it.

## Where you hear it

Daily team talk: "when is the deployment?"

## Examples

- The deployment is scheduled for Thursday morning.
- The deployment failed because an environment variable was missing.

## Common mistake

Deploying on Friday evening without a rollback plan. Deploy when people are available to fix problems.
