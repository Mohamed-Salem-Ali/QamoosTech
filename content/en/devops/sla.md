## Definition

A promise to the customer about service quality, such as "99.9% uptime" or "reply to urgent issues within 4 hours".

## Where you hear it

Contracts, support plans, and cloud providers.

## Examples

- Our SLA guarantees 99.9% uptime.
- We missed the SLA, so the client gets a refund.

## Common mistake

Promising a number you cannot measure. Always agree how the number will be tracked.

## Don't confuse with

SLA is often confused with SLO, where SLA is the formal business contract with the client, while SLO is the internal technical objective set to help achieve that agreement.

## Say it at work

- We need to review our SLA commitments before promising a four-hour response time to this new enterprise client.
- Please update the monitoring dashboard so we can track our uptime metrics against the agreed SLA.
