## Definition

An unplanned event that disrupts a service or lowers its quality, such as errors, slowness, or data problems. Teams handle it with a clear owner, a status update, and a follow-up review.

## Where you hear it

In incident channels, status pages, and postmortem reports.

## Examples

- We declared an incident when checkout errors passed 5 percent.
- The incident owner posts an update every 30 minutes.
- During the incident, the support team posted an update every half hour.

## Common mistake

Fixing the symptom quietly and never writing down what happened. The next incident then looks like a surprise.

## Don't confuse with

An incident is an event that needs a response. An outage is the period when the service is down.
