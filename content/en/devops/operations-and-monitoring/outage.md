## Definition

A period when a service is unavailable to users, either fully or for a large part of them. Outages are measured in duration and in how many users they affect.

## Where you hear it

In status pages, SLA reports, and customer messages.

## Examples

- The outage lasted 40 minutes and affected the login page.
- We will credit customers for the outage under the SLA.
- During the outage, the status page showed which regions were affected.

## Common mistake

Calling a slow service an outage, or a full outage a slowdown. The label changes how the team and the customers respond.

## Don't confuse with

An outage is a period of unavailability. An incident is the event and the response to it, which may or may not cause an outage.
