## Definition

A blameless postmortem is a written review after an incident that explains what happened, why, and how to prevent it, focusing on systems and process rather than blaming individuals.

## Where you hear it

In SRE and DevOps teams, incident follow-ups and engineering culture discussions.

## Examples

- The postmortem lists a timeline, the root cause and three action items.
- Ask what let the mistake happen, not who made it.

## Common mistake

Ending with "human error" as the cause. Ask why the system allowed that error to cause harm.

## Don't confuse with

A performance review of a person. A postmortem examines the system and has no verdict on individuals.

## Say it at work

- Let's write the postmortem by Friday.
- What would have caught this earlier?
