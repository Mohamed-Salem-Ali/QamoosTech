## Definition

Being the engineer who must respond to production alerts and incidents during a set period, often outside working hours. Teams usually rotate on-call duty.

## Where you hear it

In rotation schedules, alerting tools, and messages such as "I am on call this weekend".

## Examples

- I am on-call this week, so keep your phone close.
- On-call engineers should have a clear runbook for each alert.
- I am on-call this weekend, so I will answer the alerts from home.

## Common mistake

Putting the same person on call every week. The rotation should be fair, and each alert should be actionable.

## Don't confuse with

On-call is the duty to respond. An incident is the event that needs a response.
