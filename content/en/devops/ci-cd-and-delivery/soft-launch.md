## Definition

Releasing a product to a limited audience, such as one country or a small group of users, before the full public launch, to find problems early.

## Where you hear it

In product launch plans and in meetings about testing a market.

## Examples

- We will do a soft launch in Egypt before the regional release.
- The soft launch showed that the payment page is slow on old phones.
- The soft launch reached 200 users in Egypt for two weeks before we announced it.

## Common mistake

Calling a launch soft when it is public and advertised. A soft launch stays limited on purpose.

## Don't confuse with

A soft launch limits the audience of a finished product. A beta gives early access to testers who report problems.
