## Definition

An experiment that shows two versions of a feature to different groups of users and compares the results, such as clicks or sign-ups, to pick the better one.

## Where you hear it

In product meetings, growth discussions, and when a team says "let us test both versions".

## Examples

- The A/B test shows the new button to half of the users.
- We need enough visitors before we trust the A/B test result.
- The A/B test showed that the shorter form got 12 percent more sign-ups.

## Common mistake

Stopping the test as soon as one version looks better. Early results are often noise, so decide the sample size first.

## Don't confuse with

An A/B test compares two versions for users. A canary release checks a new build on a few servers for safety.
