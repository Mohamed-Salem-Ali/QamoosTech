## Definition

A version of software that is nearly finished and shared with testers before the final release, so they can find bugs and give feedback.

## Where you hear it

In app store listings, release announcements, and tester groups.

## Examples

- The beta is available to all testers this week.
- Please report any crash you see in the beta.
- The beta has a known problem with the export button, so we fix it before release.

## Common mistake

Treating a beta as a finished product and expecting no bugs. A beta is meant to contain bugs, and users should know that.

## Don't confuse with

A beta is shared with outside testers before release. A soft launch releases the finished product to a limited audience.
