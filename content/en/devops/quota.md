## Definition

A hard limit set by cloud providers or services on the maximum amount of a resource you can consume, such as storage space or number of API requests.

## Where you hear it

In cloud consoles, billing dashboards, and infrastructure provisioning.

## Examples

- We hit our storage quota and cannot upload any more files to the bucket.
- Please check the service quotas in the cloud dashboard before spinning up the cluster.

## Common mistake

Confusing it with rate limiting, which restricts how fast you can make requests over time rather than the total capacity allowed.
