## Definition

A domain registrar is the company where you register and renew a domain name, and which records you as the owner. It is separate from the company that hosts your website.

## Where you hear it

When buying or renewing a domain, moving a domain, and "who controls this domain?" questions.

## Examples

- We bought the domain at Namecheap and host the site on Vercel.
- Turn on auto-renew at the registrar so the domain doesn't expire.

## Common mistake

Letting the domain expire because the renewal email went to an old address. You can lose the whole site.

## Don't confuse with

A hosting provider, which runs your website. The registrar only holds the name.

## Say it at work

- Who is our registrar?
- Lock the domain against transfers.
