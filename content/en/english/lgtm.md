## Definition

Short for "Looks Good To Me". It is how a reviewer approves a pull request.

## Where you hear it

GitHub reviews and team chats.

## Examples

- LGTM. Merge when the tests are green.
- I reviewed the changes and tested the edge cases. LGTM!

## Common mistake

Writing LGTM without reading the code. That makes reviews meaningless.
