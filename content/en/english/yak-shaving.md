## Definition

A chain of small tasks you must do first, which takes you further and further from your real goal.

## Where you hear it

Developer humor and honest status updates.

## Examples

- I wanted to fix a typo, but now I am upgrading Node. Classic yak shaving.
- Stop yak shaving and go back to the original ticket.

## Common mistake

Calling needed work yak shaving. Sometimes you really must upgrade a dependency first.
