## Definition

To make a noticeable, measurable difference.

## Where you hear it

Prioritization and performance talks.

## Examples

- Will this refactor really move the needle?
- Adding an index moved the needle: the page now loads in 400 ms.

## Common mistake

Using it for tiny changes. Keep it for results that matter.
