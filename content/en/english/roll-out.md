## Definition

To release a new feature or change to users, often step by step.

## Where you hear it

Product and release discussions.

## Examples

- We will roll out the new design to 10% of users first.
- The rollout starts on Monday.

## Common mistake

Using it as a synonym for deploy. Deploying is putting code on a server; a rollout is making it available to users.
