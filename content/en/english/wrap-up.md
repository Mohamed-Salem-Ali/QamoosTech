## Definition

To finish something, such as a meeting, a task, or a sprint.

## Where you hear it

Meetings and status updates.

## Examples

- Let's wrap up the meeting; we are out of time.
- I'm wrapping up the migration script and will share it today.

## Common mistake

Saying "wrapping up" when you are only halfway. It implies you are almost done.
