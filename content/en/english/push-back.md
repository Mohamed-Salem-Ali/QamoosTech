## Definition

To express disagreement, resistance, or hesitation regarding a request, deadline, or proposed change. It is used when you need to challenge an idea or negotiate for more resources.

## Where you hear it

During project planning meetings, code reviews, or when discussing new feature requirements with stakeholders.

## Examples

- I had to push back on the deadline because the current scope is too large.
- The team pushed back on the new requirements since they would introduce significant technical debt.

## Common mistake

Using it to mean "delaying" a task. Pushing back is an act of verbal or written negotiation, not the act of postponing the work itself.

## Say it at work

- We need to push back on this feature request during the planning meeting because we do not have enough capacity.
- I pushed back on the tight deadline to ensure the team can deliver stable code without burning out.
