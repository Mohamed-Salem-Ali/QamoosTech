## Definition

To reject or dismiss an idea, proposal, or technical approach, often abruptly or during a discussion. It implies that the suggestion was not accepted for further consideration.

## Where you hear it

In design reviews, architectural meetings, or when discussing new features with a team.

## Examples

- My suggestion to rewrite the legacy module was shot down by the lead engineer.
- Don't be afraid to voice your ideas even if you think they might get shot down.

## Common mistake

Thinking it implies a physical action or a violent confrontation; in a professional setting, it simply means a proposal was rejected or deemed unfeasible.

## Don't confuse with

Shoot down vs. veto: While 'shoot down' refers to any abrupt rejection of an idea during discussion, a 'veto' is a formal, authoritative power held by a specific person to unilaterally block a decision.

## Say it at work

- I know my idea for the new API structure might get shot down, but I still think it's worth discussing.
- Please review the proposed architecture before the meeting so we can avoid having the entire plan shot down.
