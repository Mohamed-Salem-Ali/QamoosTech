## Definition

Refers to software features or solutions that function immediately after installation without requiring custom configuration or additional development. It implies a "plug-and-play" experience.

## Where you hear it

During product demos, technical sales meetings, or when evaluating third-party libraries and frameworks.

## Examples

- This framework provides out-of-the-box support for user authentication.
- We chose this tool because it offers out-of-the-box reporting features.

## Common mistake

Assuming that "out-of-the-box" means the feature is perfect for every use case; it often requires some tweaking to fit specific business logic.
