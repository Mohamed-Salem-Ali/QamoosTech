## Definition

A critical bug or technical issue that prevents software from functioning or being released. It is a problem so severe that all development must stop until it is resolved.

## Where you hear it

In project status meetings, during release planning, or when triaging high-priority bugs.

## Examples

- We cannot deploy the update because the payment gateway failure is a show-stopper.
- The team identified a show-stopper in the authentication flow that delays our launch.

## Common mistake

Using the term for minor bugs or inconveniences. A show-stopper must be a critical issue that completely prevents the core functionality of the product from working.

## Don't confuse with

A show-stopper completely halts all progress on a product release, whereas a regular blocker might only stall a specific task or dependent team.

## Say it at work

- Is this login error really a show-stopper, or can we fix it in a hotfix after release?
- Please do not merge this pull request as it introduces a show-stopper that breaks the checkout process.
