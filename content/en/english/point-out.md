## Definition

To draw attention to something, such as a problem or a better idea.

## Where you hear it

Code reviews and meetings.

## Examples

- Thanks for pointing out the missing validation.
- I'd like to point out that this query has no index.

## Common mistake

Being too blunt. Say "I noticed… could you take a look?" instead of "this is wrong".

## Say it at work

- Could you point out where the logs are stored for this microservice?
- I would like to point out that the current implementation may cause a memory leak under high load.
