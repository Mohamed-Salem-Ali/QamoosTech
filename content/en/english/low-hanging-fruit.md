## Definition

Tasks that are easy to do and give quick value, so you do them first.

## Where you hear it

Planning and prioritization.

## Examples

- Let's fix the low-hanging fruit before the big redesign.
- Compressing images is low-hanging fruit for page speed.
- Adding missing alt text to the images is low-hanging fruit that improves accessibility.

## Common mistake

Doing only the easy things and never the important hard ones.

## Say it at work

- We should tackle the low-hanging fruit in the backlog to show some progress to the stakeholders.
- I have identified a few low-hanging fruit items that we can implement in the current sprint to improve performance.
