## Definition

Refers to the internal, hidden implementation details of a library, framework, or system that developers usually do not need to see or modify.

## Where you hear it

In documentation, architecture discussions, and code reviews when explaining how a feature works beyond its public API.

## Examples

- The framework handles routing automatically under the hood.
- Let's look under the hood to see how the query builder parses SQL.

## Common mistake

Thinking you must understand everything under the hood before you can use a tool effectively.

## Don't confuse with

Under the hood refers to the internal implementation details, while abstraction hides those details behind a simple interface so you do not have to worry about them.

## Say it at work

- I am curious, how does this state management library handle updates under the hood?
- The pull request description explains what happens under the hood when the new caching layer is enabled.
