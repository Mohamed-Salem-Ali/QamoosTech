## Definition

To allocate a fixed, maximum amount of time for a specific task to prevent over-engineering and endless discussions.

## Where you hear it

In planning sessions, agile meetings, and when managing project scope.

## Examples

- We will time-box this architectural discussion to thirty minutes so we can move on to other tasks.
- Let us time-box the research phase of this feature to two days.

## Common mistake

Treating a time-box as a strict target or goal instead of a hard limit where you must stop and make a decision.
