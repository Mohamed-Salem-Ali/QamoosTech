## Definition

A phrase meaning "release it now". It encourages the team to stop polishing and deliver.

## Where you hear it

Team chat and reviews.

## Examples

- The tests pass and the review is done. Ship it!
- Ship it, then improve it in the next version.

## Common mistake

Using it to skip tests or review. "Ship it" means the work is ready, not that checks are optional.

## Don't confuse with

Ship it is often confused with 'deploy'. While 'deploy' refers to the technical process of moving code to a server, 'ship it' is a broader decision to release the feature to end users.

## Say it at work

- The final bug fix is merged, so let's ship it and move on to the next sprint.
- I have verified the staging environment and everything looks stable, so we are ready to ship it.
