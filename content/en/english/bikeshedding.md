## Definition

Spending too much time on small, easy details while ignoring the important decisions.

## Where you hear it

Meetings and code reviews.

## Examples

- We spent 20 minutes bikeshedding about a variable name.
- Let's avoid bikeshedding and decide on the database first.

## Common mistake

Using the word to end any discussion. Naming matters; the problem is wasting time out of proportion.
