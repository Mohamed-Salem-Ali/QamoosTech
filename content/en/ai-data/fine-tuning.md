## Definition

Training an existing AI model a little more on your own examples so it performs better on your specific task.

## Where you hear it

Customizing AI models.

## Examples

- We fine-tuned the model on 1,000 labeled support emails.
- Try a better prompt before you consider fine-tuning.

## Common mistake

Jumping to fine-tuning too early. A good prompt or RAG is often cheaper and enough.

## Don't confuse with

Fine-tuning is often confused with RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation); while fine-tuning alters the model's internal weights to change its behavior, RAG provides external context to the model at runtime without changing the model itself.

## Say it at work

- Do we really need fine-tuning here, or can we just improve our system prompt to get better results?
- I have prepared the dataset for fine-tuning the model to better recognize our specific domain terminology.
