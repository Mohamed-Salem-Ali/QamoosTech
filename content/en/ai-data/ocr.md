## Definition

Technology that reads text from images or scanned documents and turns it into text a computer can use.

## Where you hear it

Document scanning, invoices, and forms.

## Examples

- The OCR could not read the blurry photo.
- We run OCR on every uploaded invoice.

## Common mistake

Expecting perfect results from any image. Quality depends on the image, so clean it first.

## Don't confuse with

OCR extracts text from images, while ICR (Intelligent Character Recognition) goes a step further by recognizing handwriting and different fonts using machine learning.

## Say it at work

- Can we check if the OCR service handles multi-language receipts properly?
- Please ensure the uploaded documents have sufficient resolution for the OCR pipeline to process them accurately.
