## Definition

Technology that reads text from images or scanned documents and turns it into text a computer can use.

## Where you hear it

Document scanning, invoices, and forms.

## Examples

- The OCR could not read the blurry photo.
- We run OCR on every uploaded invoice.

## Common mistake

Expecting perfect results from any image. Quality depends on the image, so clean it first.
