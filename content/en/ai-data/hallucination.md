## Definition

When an AI model confidently says something false or invented, such as a fake source or a function that does not exist.

## Where you hear it

Any discussion about trusting AI output.

## Examples

- The model hallucinated a function that does not exist in the library.
- Always check the sources, because the answer may be a hallucination.

## Common mistake

Copying AI-generated code without running it. Test everything the model writes.
