## Definition

A learning method where you review something at growing intervals, right before you would forget it, so it stays in memory.

## Where you hear it

Flashcard apps like Anki and learning products.

## Examples

- Use spaced repetition to memorize these new terms.
- The app shows hard cards more often.

## Common mistake

Cramming everything in one day. Short reviews spread over days work much better.

## Don't confuse with

Spaced repetition is often confused with active recall; while spaced repetition focuses on the timing of reviews, active recall focuses on the process of retrieving information from memory without looking at the source.
