## Definition

Writing clear instructions (prompts) with context and examples so an AI model gives accurate, consistent answers.

## Where you hear it

Building AI features and using AI tools daily.

## Examples

- Better prompt engineering reduced wrong answers.
- Give the model two examples of the output format you want.

## Common mistake

Writing vague prompts and blaming the model. Be specific about the task, the format, and the limits.

## Don't confuse with

Prompt engineering is often confused with prompt injection; the former is the constructive process of optimizing inputs to guide a model, while the latter is a security vulnerability where an attacker manipulates inputs to bypass model constraints.

## Say it at work

- I've been tweaking our prompt engineering to make the chatbot's tone more consistent with our brand guidelines.
- Please review the updated prompt engineering documentation to ensure our system prompts follow the new security standards.
