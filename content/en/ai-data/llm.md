## Definition

An AI model trained on huge amounts of text that can understand and write language, answer questions, and generate code.

## Where you hear it

AI features, chatbots, and developer tools.

## Examples

- We use an LLM to summarize support tickets.
- The LLM returned a different answer the second time.

## Common mistake

Treating its answer as always correct or always the same. Validate important outputs.
