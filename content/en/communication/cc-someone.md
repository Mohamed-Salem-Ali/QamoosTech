## Definition

To include someone on an email thread so they can read the conversation for visibility without being the primary recipient. It comes from the older office practice of using carbon paper to make copies of typed letters.

## Where you hear it

In team syncs, email requests, and project update discussions when discussing who needs to stay informed about an ongoing issue.

## Examples

- CC me on the email to the client so I can track the request.
- I will CC the manager on this thread to keep them updated on the bug fix.

## Common mistake

Thinking that CCing someone means they are required to take action or reply, whereas it is usually just for their awareness and visibility.

## Don't confuse with

CCing someone keeps them informed for visibility, while BCCing them hides their email address from all other recipients on the thread.

## Say it at work

- Could you please CC me on that email chain so I can track the deployment updates?
- Please ensure to CC the QA lead on all bug reports regarding the payment gateway.
