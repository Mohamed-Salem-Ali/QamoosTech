## Definition

An early warning about something that may affect someone, so they can prepare.

## Where you hear it

Chat and team updates.

## Examples

- Quick heads-up: the server will be down at 2 AM for maintenance.
- Thanks for the heads-up!

## Common mistake

Giving the heads-up too late. It only helps when there is still time to react.
