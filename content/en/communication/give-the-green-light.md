## Definition

To give formal approval or authorization to start a project, feature, or deployment. It signals that all necessary requirements have been met and the team is cleared to proceed.

## Where you hear it

Used in project meetings, status updates, or via email when waiting for a manager or client to approve a plan.

## Examples

- The manager finally gave the green light to deploy the new feature to production.
- We are waiting for the client to give the green light before we start the next phase.

## Common mistake

Thinking it means the task is already finished; it only means you have permission to begin working on it or to release it.
