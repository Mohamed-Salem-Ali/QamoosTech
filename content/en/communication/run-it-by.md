## Definition

To share an idea, design, or plan with someone to get their feedback, opinion, or approval before taking action.

## Where you hear it

In team meetings, chat channels, or quick syncs before merging code or changing system architecture.

## Examples

- Let me run this database migration plan by the senior engineer before we apply it.
- I have a new caching strategy, but I want to run it by the team lead first.

## Common mistake

Thinking it means asking for permission to do basic work, when it is usually just a quick sanity check to prevent unexpected issues.
