## Definition

The expected time when something will be ready or finished.

## Where you hear it

Status updates and incident chats.

## Examples

- What's the ETA for the fix?
- ETA is about two hours.

## Common mistake

Giving an ETA without checking, then going silent when it changes. Update people early.
