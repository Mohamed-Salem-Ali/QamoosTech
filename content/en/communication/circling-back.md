## Definition

A polite way to say you are returning to a topic that was postponed or left without an answer.

## Where you hear it

Work emails and meetings.

## Examples

- I'm circling back on the pricing question from Monday.
- Let's circle back to this after the demo.

## Common mistake

Using it too often as a way to avoid deciding. Say what decision you need.
