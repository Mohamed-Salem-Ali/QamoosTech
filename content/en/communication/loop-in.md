## Definition

To add someone to a conversation or email so they know what is happening.

## Where you hear it

Emails and chat threads.

## Examples

- I'm looping in Ahmed, who owns the billing service.
- Please loop me in on the client thread.

## Common mistake

Looping in too many people. Add only those who need to act or decide.

## Don't confuse with

Loop in is often confused with CCing someone; while looping in implies active inclusion for collaboration or decision-making, CCing is often used just to keep someone passively informed.

## Say it at work

- I'll loop in the DevOps lead so we can get their input on the infrastructure changes.
- Please loop in the project manager on this thread to ensure they are aware of the current blockers.
