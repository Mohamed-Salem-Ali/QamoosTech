## Definition

To add someone to a conversation or email so they know what is happening.

## Where you hear it

Emails and chat threads.

## Examples

- I'm looping in Ahmed, who owns the billing service.
- Please loop me in on the client thread.

## Common mistake

Looping in too many people. Add only those who need to act or decide.
