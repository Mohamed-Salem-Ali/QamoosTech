## Definition

A casual way to ask someone to send a quick message or notification via chat.

## Where you hear it

In team chat apps like Slack or Microsoft Teams when coordinating tasks.

## Examples

- Ping me when the deployment finishes.
- Can you ping me the link to the documentation?

## Common mistake

Treating it as a formal email instead of a quick chat message.

## Don't confuse with

Ping me is often confused with 'call me', but pinging refers specifically to a short, asynchronous text notification, whereas calling implies a real-time synchronous voice or video conversation.

## Say it at work

- Just ping me on Slack once you've had a chance to review the PR.
- Please ping me when the staging environment is ready for testing.
