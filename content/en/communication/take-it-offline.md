## Definition

To move a discussion from a group meeting or public channel to a private, one-on-one conversation. This is usually done to save time for others when a topic becomes too specific or lengthy.

## Where you hear it

During team meetings, video conferences, or public Slack channels.

## Examples

- This sounds like a specific edge case; let's take it offline.
- I think we are getting into too much detail here, let's take it offline.

## Common mistake

Thinking that "offline" means literally disconnecting from the internet or stopping work, rather than just changing the venue of the conversation.
