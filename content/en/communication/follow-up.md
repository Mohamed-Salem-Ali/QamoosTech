## Definition

To contact someone again about something you already asked or discussed, to check progress or get an answer.

## Where you hear it

Emails, chat messages, and meeting notes.

## Examples

- I'm following up on the access request I sent last week.
- Just a quick follow-up: did you get a chance to review the PR?

## Common mistake

Following up too many times in one day. One polite reminder after two or three days is enough.
