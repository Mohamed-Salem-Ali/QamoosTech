## Definition

A polite, professional phrase used to invite feedback or opinions from colleagues regarding a specific document, code change, or proposal.

## Where you hear it

In pull request comments, Slack messages, or email threads when seeking input.

## Examples

- I have updated the documentation; let me know your thoughts.
- Here is the draft for the new API design, let me know your thoughts.

## Common mistake

Using it when you actually need a formal approval, as this phrase implies a casual request for feedback rather than a required sign-off.
