## Definition

A message or status that tells people you are away and when you will be back.

## Where you hear it

Vacations and sick days.

## Examples

- I'm out of office until Monday, so please contact Sara for urgent issues.
- His auto-reply says he is OOO this week.

## Common mistake

Setting an out-of-office message with no backup contact. Always name who can help.
