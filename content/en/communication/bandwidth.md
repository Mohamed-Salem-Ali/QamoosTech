## Definition

In work talk, the amount of time and energy a person has available for new tasks.

## Where you hear it

Planning and workload talks.

## Examples

- I don't have the bandwidth to take another project this month.
- Do you have the bandwidth to review this PR today?

## Common mistake

Confusing it with internet bandwidth. In work meetings it usually means free time and energy.

## Don't confuse with

Bandwidth is often confused with capacity; while bandwidth refers to the available time and energy to take on new tasks, capacity refers to the total volume of work a team or system can handle over a specific period.

## Say it at work

- I'm currently at full capacity, so I don't think I have the bandwidth to jump into that new feature right now.
- Please let me know if you have the bandwidth to assist with these bug fixes before the end of the sprint.
