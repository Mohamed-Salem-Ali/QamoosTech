## Definition

A request for someone to continue providing updates as a task or situation progresses.

## Where you hear it

In Slack messages, daily standups, and status update emails.

## Examples

- Keep me posted on how the deployment goes tonight.
- Keep me posted if you find any new bugs during testing.

## Common mistake

Thinking it means a final report is needed right away, rather than waiting for future developments.
