## Definition

The list of topics to discuss in a meeting, shared in advance.

## Where you hear it

Meeting invitations.

## Examples

- I'll send the agenda a day before the meeting.
- Let's stick to the agenda so we finish in 30 minutes.

## Common mistake

Calling a meeting with no agenda. People arrive unprepared and time is wasted.

## Don't confuse with

Agenda is often confused with 'minutes'; while an agenda outlines the topics to be discussed before a meeting, minutes are the official record of what was actually decided during the session.

## Say it at work

- Could you please add this topic to the agenda before we start the sync?
- I have attached the agenda for tomorrow's review session to the calendar invite.
