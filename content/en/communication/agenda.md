## Definition

The list of topics to discuss in a meeting, shared in advance.

## Where you hear it

Meeting invitations.

## Examples

- I'll send the agenda a day before the meeting.
- Let's stick to the agenda so we finish in 30 minutes.

## Common mistake

Calling a meeting with no agenda. People arrive unprepared and time is wasted.
