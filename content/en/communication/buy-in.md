## Definition

Buy-in refers to the process of gaining agreement, commitment, or support from stakeholders or team members for a specific technical decision or project. It ensures that everyone involved is aligned and willing to contribute to the success of the initiative.

## Where you hear it

In project planning meetings, architectural reviews, or when proposing a new tool or process to management.

## Examples

- We need to get buy-in from the engineering team before we switch to a new database.
- The proposal failed because we couldn't get buy-in from the product managers.

## Common mistake

Thinking that buy-in is the same as simply informing people of a decision; it actually requires active engagement and consensus-building to ensure stakeholders feel heard and invested in the outcome.
