## Definition

A polite, professional phrase used to request that someone completes a task as soon as they are able to, without imposing a strict or urgent deadline.

## Where you hear it

Used in emails, Slack messages, or project management tickets when requesting a review or a non-urgent update.

## Examples

- Please review the pull request at your earliest convenience.
- Could you share the updated documentation at your earliest convenience?

## Common mistake

Using this phrase when a task is actually urgent; it can be misinterpreted as "whenever you feel like it," which might lead to delays if the recipient assumes it is low priority.
