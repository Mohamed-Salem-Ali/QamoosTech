## Definition

A polite way to say you are letting someone else make the final decision based on their expertise.

## Where you hear it

In meetings, code reviews, and planning discussions when domain knowledge rests with a teammate.

## Examples

- I defer to Sarah on the database schema design.
- Let's defer to the security team regarding the authentication flow.

## Common mistake

Thinking it means you disagree completely, when it actually means you trust another person's judgment and step back.

## Say it at work

- I defer to you on the API rate limiting strategy, so let me know how you want to proceed.
- We should defer to the frontend lead regarding the state management library selection.
