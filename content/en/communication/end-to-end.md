## Definition

Covering the whole process from the first step to the last, with no part left to someone else.

## Where you hear it

CVs, interviews, and project descriptions.

## Examples

- I built the feature end-to-end, from design to deployment.
- We need an end-to-end test of the checkout flow.

## Common mistake

Claiming "end-to-end" when you did only a part. Interviewers ask follow-up questions.
