## Definition

A phrase that points to something you already wrote. It can sound a little cold or annoyed, so use it carefully.

## Where you hear it

Work emails, often when information was missed.

## Examples

- As mentioned in my previous email, the deadline is Friday.
- Just to restate: the files are attached to my earlier message.

## Common mistake

Using "per my last email" when you are annoyed. A softer version is "As mentioned earlier…" or "Just to confirm…".
