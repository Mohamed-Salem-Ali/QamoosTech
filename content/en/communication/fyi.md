## Definition

Short for "for your information". You send it when you want someone to know something, without asking them to do anything.

## Where you hear it

In emails, chat messages, and ticket comments.

## Examples

- FYI, the release moved to Tuesday.
- FYI only, no action needed from you.
- FYI, the staging database was reset last night, so the old test data is gone.

## Common mistake

Using FYI for something that needs a reply. If you need an answer or a decision, ask a clear question.

## Don't confuse with

FYI shares information. A request asks someone to act. Mixing them makes people miss the request.
