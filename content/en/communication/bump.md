## Definition

To remind someone about something that is waiting, such as a review or a reply, by raising it again.

## Where you hear it

In chat threads and pull request comments, when a review has been waiting for days.

## Examples

- Bumping this PR, it has been waiting since Monday.
- A quick bump usually gets a faster review.
- I bumped the dependency to version 4.2 to fix the security warning.

## Common mistake

Bumping every hour. One polite reminder after a reasonable wait is enough.

## Don't confuse with

A bump reminds someone about a request that is waiting. A follow-up continues a discussion with new information.
