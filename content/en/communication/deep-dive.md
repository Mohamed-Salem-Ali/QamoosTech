## Definition

A thorough and detailed examination or discussion of a specific technical topic, architecture, or problem.

## Where you hear it

In architecture reviews, team meetings, and complex technical planning sessions.

## Examples

- We need to schedule a deep dive into our authentication system next week.
- Let us do a deep dive on this performance bottleneck during the afternoon session.

## Common mistake

Using it for a quick status update or a surface-level overview instead of an intensive, detailed analysis.
