## Definition

A specific task that someone agreed to do after a meeting, usually with a name and a date.

## Where you hear it

Meeting notes and project tools.

## Examples

- Action item: Sara will send the updated design by Wednesday.
- I'll email everyone the action items after the call.

## Common mistake

Ending a meeting without owners and dates. Then nothing happens.
