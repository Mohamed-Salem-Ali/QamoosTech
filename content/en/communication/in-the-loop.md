## Definition

Being actively included in communications, updates, and decision-making processes for a project.

## Where you hear it

Project status meetings, email threads, and daily standups.

## Examples

- Please keep me in the loop regarding any changes to the API schema.
- She is in the loop on all major architecture decisions.

## Common mistake

Thinking it means doing the actual work, rather than simply being kept informed and copied on updates.
