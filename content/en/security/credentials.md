## Definition

Credentials are the data, such as usernames and passwords, used to verify the identity of a user or a system. They act as proof that an entity is who they claim to be before granting access to resources.

## Where you hear it

Used during user authentication flows, API integration setup, and security audits.

## Examples

- Please ensure you do not hardcode your database credentials in the source code.
- The application requires valid credentials to access the protected endpoint.

## Common mistake

Confusing credentials with permissions; credentials prove who you are, while permissions determine what you are allowed to do after you have been identified.
