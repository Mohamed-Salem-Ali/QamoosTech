## Definition

Recording who did what and when in a system, so you can investigate problems and prove compliance.

## Where you hear it

Healthcare, finance, and security reviews.

## Examples

- The audit log shows who changed the patient record.
- Audit logs must be append-only.

## Common mistake

Allowing audit logs to be edited or deleted. Then nobody can trust them.
