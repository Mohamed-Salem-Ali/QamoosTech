## Definition

SOC 2 is an audit report, produced by an independent auditor, that shows a service company has controls for security, availability and the protection of customer data. Enterprise customers often require it.

## Where you hear it

In sales and security questionnaires, SaaS vendor reviews and compliance projects.

## Examples

- The customer asked for our SOC 2 report before signing.
- Type 2 covers how controls worked over several months.
- Our SOC 2 Type 2 report covers six months of controls and is shared under an NDA.

## Common mistake

Thinking it is a certificate you pass once. It is a report on a period, and controls must keep working.

## Don't confuse with

ISO 27001, another security standard that certifies a management system rather than reporting on controls.

## Say it at work

- Do you have a SOC 2 Type 2?
- We start the audit window next quarter.
