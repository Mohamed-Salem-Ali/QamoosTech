## Definition

Removing a person's personal data when they ask, as required by the European GDPR law ("right to be forgotten").

## Where you hear it

Privacy, legal reviews, and account deletion features.

## Examples

- The user asked us to delete their account under GDPR.
- We must also remove the data from backups and analytics.

## Common mistake

Deleting only from the main database and forgetting caches, backups, search indexes, and third-party tools.
