## Definition

Any data that can identify a person: name, phone number, email, national ID, address.

## Where you hear it

Privacy policies, security reviews, and data handling rules.

## Examples

- Do not log any PII.
- Mask the PII before sending data to analytics.

## Common mistake

Thinking only names are PII. Emails, phone numbers, and IP addresses can be PII too.
