## Definition

A specific thing you promise to hand over to the client, such as a deployed website, a design file, or documentation.

## Where you hear it

Proposals, contracts, and project updates.

## Examples

- The main deliverable for phase one is a working admin dashboard.
- Please list the deliverables in the proposal.

## Common mistake

Writing vague deliverables like "build the backend". Say exactly what the client will receive.
