## Definition

Client onboarding is the structured process of gathering necessary information, credentials, and legal agreements from a new client to begin a project. It ensures that both the freelancer and the client are aligned on expectations and have the access required to start working.

## Where you hear it

In project management meetings, freelance business planning, and client communication.

## Examples

- We need to complete the client onboarding before we can start the development phase.
- I have a standard checklist to streamline my client onboarding process.

## Common mistake

Skipping the onboarding process to start coding immediately, which often leads to delays later when you realize you lack the necessary access or project requirements.

## Don't confuse with

Client onboarding is the initial setup process for a new client, while client handoff is the final process of delivering the completed project back to them.

## Say it at work

- Let us schedule a quick call to go through the client onboarding and get all the required access sorted.
- Please find attached the welcome package and agreement needed to finalize the client onboarding for the new project.
