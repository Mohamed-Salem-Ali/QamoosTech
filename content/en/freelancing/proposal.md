## Definition

A document or message where you explain how you will solve the client's problem, how long it will take, and the price.

## Where you hear it

Freelance platforms and client leads.

## Examples

- I sent the client a proposal with three milestones.
- Your proposal should answer the client's exact problem.

## Common mistake

Sending the same copied proposal to everyone. Mention the client's real problem in the first lines.
