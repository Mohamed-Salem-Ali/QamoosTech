## Definition

A formal request to change the agreed scope, usually with a new estimate for time and price.

## Where you hear it

Client projects after the contract is signed.

## Examples

- This new feature needs a change request.
- I approved the change request and updated the timeline.

## Common mistake

Making changes first and talking about price later. Agree on cost and time before you start.
