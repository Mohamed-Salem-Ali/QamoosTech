## Definition

Tasks, features, or requests that fall outside the agreed-upon boundaries of a project.

## Where you hear it

In client meetings, proposal discussions, and when reviewing new feature requests.

## Examples

- Adding user authentication is out of scope for this initial landing page project.
- The client asked for a mobile app, but that request is out of scope.

## Common mistake

Thinking you must complete extra requests for free just to keep the client happy, instead of discussing a change request or extra cost.
