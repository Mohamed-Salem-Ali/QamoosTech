## Definition

A regular monthly payment that reserves a set amount of your time or availability for a client.

## Where you hear it

Long-term client work and maintenance contracts.

## Examples

- The client pays a monthly retainer for ten hours of support.
- A retainer gives me stable income.
- The retainer covers twenty hours a month, and extra hours are billed separately.

## Common mistake

Not defining what the retainer covers. Write the hours, the tasks, and the response time.

## Don't confuse with

Retainer is often confused with a subscription; a retainer reserves a specific amount of your professional time for a client, whereas a subscription typically grants access to a pre-built product or service.

## Say it at work

- I'm currently full, but I can open up a spot if you're interested in moving to a monthly retainer.
- Please find the updated contract attached, which outlines the specific scope of work covered by our monthly retainer agreement.
