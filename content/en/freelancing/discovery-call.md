## Definition

A discovery call is an initial meeting between a freelancer and a potential client to discuss project requirements, goals, and expectations. It helps determine if both parties are a good fit before committing to a formal agreement.

## Where you hear it

Commonly used during the sales process, client onboarding, or when responding to new project inquiries.

## Examples

- I have a discovery call scheduled with a new lead tomorrow morning.
- We should hold a discovery call to better understand the project scope.
- On the discovery call, the client explained that they need the app in three weeks.

## Common mistake

Treating the discovery call as a technical consultation where you provide free solutions, rather than using it as a qualification step to assess the client's needs and budget.

## Say it at work

- Let us schedule a quick discovery call to discuss your requirements before sending the estimate.
- Please find attached the notes from today's discovery call along with the proposed next steps.
