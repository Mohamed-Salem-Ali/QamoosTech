## Definition

A legal agreement where you promise not to share the client's private information, code, or business plans.

## Where you hear it

Starting a new client or company project.

## Examples

- I can't show that code because of an NDA.
- The client asked us to sign an NDA before the call.

## Common mistake

Posting client work in your public portfolio. Ask for permission or hide the private details.
