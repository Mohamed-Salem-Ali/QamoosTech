## Definition

A formal contractual document that outlines project deliverables, timelines, milestones, and payment terms between a client and a service provider.

## Where you hear it

In contract negotiations, project kickoff meetings, and scoping discussions with clients.

## Examples

- The client requested a detailed Statement of Work before signing the contract.
- We added an extra phase to the Statement of Work to cover the new features.

## Common mistake

Treating the Statement of Work as a flexible guideline rather than a legally binding document.
