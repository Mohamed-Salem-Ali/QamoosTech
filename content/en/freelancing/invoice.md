## Definition

A document you send to ask for payment, listing the work done, the amount, and the due date.

## Where you hear it

Freelancing and client billing.

## Examples

- I will send the invoice after the delivery.
- The invoice is due within 14 days.

## Common mistake

Sending invoices without clear payment terms. State the due date and the payment method.
