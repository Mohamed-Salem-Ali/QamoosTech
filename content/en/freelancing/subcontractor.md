## Definition

A person or company hired by a primary contractor to perform specific tasks or services for a larger project. The subcontractor reports to the primary contractor rather than the end client.

## Where you hear it

In project management discussions, contract negotiations, or when a freelancer needs additional expertise to meet a project deadline.

## Examples

- I am hiring a designer as a subcontractor to help with the UI elements of this project.
- The contract allows me to use a subcontractor for the backend development if necessary.

## Common mistake

Assuming the client has a direct legal or professional relationship with the subcontractor, when in fact the primary contractor is solely responsible for the subcontractor's work.
