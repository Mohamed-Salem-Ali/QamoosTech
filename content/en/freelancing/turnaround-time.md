## Definition

The time between receiving a request and delivering the result.

## Where you hear it

Support work and client updates.

## Examples

- My usual turnaround time is two working days.
- For urgent bugs, our turnaround time is under 24 hours.

## Common mistake

Promising a very fast turnaround without a buffer. Delays then damage trust.
