## Definition

*Fixed-price* means one agreed price for a defined job. *Hourly* means the client pays for the hours you actually work.

## Where you hear it

Upwork-style proposals and client onboarding.

## Examples

- For unclear requirements, I recommend an hourly contract.
- The fixed-price quote covers exactly these five features.

## Common mistake

Accepting a fixed price when the requirements are unclear. You risk working for a very low hourly rate.
