## Definition

An important checkpoint in a project, often linked to a deliverable and a payment.

## Where you hear it

Contracts, proposals, and project plans.

## Examples

- The first milestone is the working login, with 30% payment.
- We reached the second milestone on time.

## Common mistake

Making milestones too big. Smaller milestones make progress visible and reduce payment risk.
