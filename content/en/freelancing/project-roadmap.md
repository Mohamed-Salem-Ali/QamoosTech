## Definition

A high-level timeline showing major phases and feature rollouts for the client.

## Where you hear it

During project planning, client pitch meetings, and scope discussions.

## Examples

- We shared the project roadmap with the client before signing the contract.
- The project roadmap outlines the deliverables for each quarter.

## Common mistake

Confusing it with a detailed daily task list or a rigid schedule that cannot change.

## Don't confuse with

Project Roadmap vs. Project Plan: A roadmap focuses on high-level strategic goals and milestones, whereas a project plan details the specific tasks, resources, and dependencies required to execute those goals.

## Say it at work

- Let's take a look at the project roadmap so we can align on the major milestones for the upcoming months.
- I have attached the updated project roadmap to this ticket for your review before we proceed with the next phase.
