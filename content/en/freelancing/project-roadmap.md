## Definition

A high-level timeline showing major phases and feature rollouts for the client.

## Where you hear it

During project planning, client pitch meetings, and scope discussions.

## Examples

- We shared the project roadmap with the client before signing the contract.
- The project roadmap outlines the deliverables for each quarter.

## Common mistake

Confusing it with a detailed daily task list or a rigid schedule that cannot change.
