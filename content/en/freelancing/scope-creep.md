## Definition

When a project keeps growing with new requests, but the time and the price stay the same.

## Where you hear it

Freelance projects and fixed-price contracts.

## Examples

- Three extra screens is scope creep. Let's send a change request.
- To avoid scope creep, we wrote the scope in the contract.
- The client added a dashboard, a report and a login page without changing the price.

## Common mistake

Saying yes to "small" extras without a record. Small extras add up quickly.

## Don't confuse with

Scope creep is often confused with feature creep; while scope creep refers to unauthorized additions that impact the project's budget and timeline, feature creep refers to the tendency of developers to add excessive, unnecessary features to a product regardless of the project constraints.

## Say it at work

- If we keep adding these minor tweaks without adjusting the deadline, we are going to run into serious scope creep.
- I have reviewed the new requirements and believe they fall outside our initial agreement, which could lead to scope creep if not addressed formally.
