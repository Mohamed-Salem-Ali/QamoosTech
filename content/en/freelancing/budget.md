## Definition

The total amount a client has agreed to spend on a project, or the amount a freelancer has planned for the work.

## Where you hear it

In proposals, scope discussions, and when a client asks "What is the budget?"

## Examples

- The budget covers design and two rounds of revisions.
- We are over budget because the scope grew.
- The client raised the budget, so we can add the mobile version.

## Common mistake

Accepting a project without asking about the budget. An estimate without a budget can lead to unpaid work.

## Don't confuse with

A budget is the money available. An estimate is the expected cost of the work in hours or money.

## Say it at work

- What is the budget range for this project?
