## Definition

A legally binding agreement between a freelancer and a client that defines the project scope, payment terms, deadlines, and intellectual property rights.

## Where you hear it

In client onboarding, before starting any development work, or when discussing project terms.

## Examples

- Always sign a freelance contract before writing any code for a new client.
- The freelance contract specifies the hourly rate and the total number of revision rounds.

## Common mistake

Starting a project based only on a chat message or verbal agreement without a signed contract, which leads to unpaid work and scope creep.

## Don't confuse with

Freelance contract vs. Statement of Work (SOW): A freelance contract is the overarching legal agreement governing the relationship, while an SOW is a specific document detailing the technical tasks, deliverables, and timelines for a particular project.

## Say it at work

- Could you send over the freelance contract so I can review the payment terms before we kick off?
- I have attached the signed freelance contract for your records; please let me know if you need any further documentation.
