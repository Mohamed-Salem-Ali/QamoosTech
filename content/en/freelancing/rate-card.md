## Definition

A document or table that lists your prices for various software development services, hourly rates, or project packages.

## Where you hear it

Freelance discussions, client onboarding, and contract negotiations.

## Examples

- I updated my rate card to reflect my new senior developer hourly fee.
- The client asked for a rate card before discussing the backend migration project.
- The rate card lists the hourly rate for design, development and QA.

## Common mistake

Treating a rate card as a rigid contract rather than a starting point for pricing discussions.

## Don't confuse with

A rate card is a general price list for services, while an estimate is a calculated cost prediction for a specific, scoped project.

## Say it at work

- Take a look at my rate card and let me know which package fits your budget.
- Please find attached our latest rate card for custom software development services.
