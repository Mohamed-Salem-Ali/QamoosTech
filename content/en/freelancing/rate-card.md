## Definition

A document or table that lists your prices for various software development services, hourly rates, or project packages.

## Where you hear it

Freelance discussions, client onboarding, and contract negotiations.

## Examples

- I updated my rate card to reflect my new senior developer hourly fee.
- The client asked for a rate card before discussing the backend migration project.

## Common mistake

Treating a rate card as a rigid contract rather than a starting point for pricing discussions.
