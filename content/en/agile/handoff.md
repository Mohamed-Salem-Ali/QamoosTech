## Definition

Passing work to another person or team, with the information they need to continue it.

## Where you hear it

Team changes, client delivery, and design-to-development work.

## Examples

- I wrote a handoff document before going on leave.
- The design handoff includes the colors and spacing.

## Common mistake

Handing off with no documentation. The next person then has to guess.
