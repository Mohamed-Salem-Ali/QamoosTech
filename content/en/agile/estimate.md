## Definition

A careful guess of how much time or effort a task will need. It is not a promise.

## Where you hear it

Planning and client proposals.

## Examples

- My estimate is three days, including testing.
- Can you give me a rough estimate?

## Common mistake

Estimating without checking the unknowns. Add buffer for what you do not know yet.

## Don't confuse with

Estimate vs. Deadline: An estimate is a calculated prediction of the effort required for a task, whereas a deadline is a fixed date by which the work must be completed.

## Say it at work

- Let's refine our estimate for this feature once we have a better understanding of the API requirements.
- I have attached an updated estimate to the ticket based on the latest feedback from the design team.
