## Definition

A careful guess of how much time or effort a task will need. It is not a promise.

## Where you hear it

Planning and client proposals.

## Examples

- My estimate is three days, including testing.
- Can you give me a rough estimate?

## Common mistake

Estimating without checking the unknowns. Add buffer for what you do not know yet.
