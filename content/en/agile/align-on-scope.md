## Definition

Agreeing clearly, before starting, on what is included in the work and what is not.

## Where you hear it

Kickoff meetings and client talks.

## Examples

- Let's align on scope before we write any code.
- We aligned on scope: the admin panel is phase two.

## Common mistake

Agreeing only in a call. Write the agreement down, even in a short message.

## Don't confuse with

Align on scope vs. define requirements: Aligning on scope focuses on setting boundaries for what is in or out of the project, while defining requirements details the specific functional and technical specifications needed to build the solution.

## Say it at work

- Can we quickly align on scope for this sprint so we don't end up over-delivering?
- Please review the attached document to ensure we are fully aligned on scope before the development phase begins.
