## Definition

Agreeing clearly, before starting, on what is included in the work and what is not.

## Where you hear it

Kickoff meetings and client talks.

## Examples

- Let's align on scope before we write any code.
- We aligned on scope: the admin panel is phase two.

## Common mistake

Agreeing only in a call. Write the agreement down, even in a short message.
