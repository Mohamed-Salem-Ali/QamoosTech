## Definition

A short session where a team shows working software to stakeholders, usually at the end of a sprint, so they can give feedback.

## Where you hear it

In sprint reviews, client meetings, and team channels, when someone asks "Can you show us how it works?"

## Examples

- We will do a demo of the new checkout flow on Thursday.
- The demo failed because the staging database was empty.
- Before the demo, we tested the checkout flow on the staging server.

## Common mistake

Showing slides or mockups and calling it a demo. A demo shows the software actually running.

## Don't confuse with

A demo shows finished work to others. A test checks that the work behaves correctly. A demo can happen without any tests.

## Say it at work

- Can we do a quick demo before we merge?
- The demo is scheduled for Friday at 3 PM.
