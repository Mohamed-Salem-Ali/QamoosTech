## Definition

A short, fixed period (usually one or two weeks) in which a team completes a planned amount of work.

## Where you hear it

Agile and Scrum teams.

## Examples

- We plan to finish the login feature in this sprint.
- Let's move this task to the next sprint.

## Common mistake

Adding new work in the middle of a sprint. That breaks the plan and the team's focus.
