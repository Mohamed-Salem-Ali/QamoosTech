## Definition

Capacity refers to the total amount of work a team can realistically complete during a specific period, such as a sprint. It is usually calculated based on the team's available time and historical velocity.

## Where you hear it

During sprint planning meetings or when discussing team availability with stakeholders.

## Examples

- We need to check our capacity before committing to these new user stories.
- Our team's capacity is reduced this sprint due to upcoming holidays.
- We have capacity for two medium tasks, but not for the whole redesign.

## Common mistake

Confusing capacity with velocity; capacity is about the available time and resources, while velocity is the actual amount of work the team historically completes.

## Don't confuse with

Capacity measures available time and resources, whereas velocity measures the actual amount of work the team historically completes.

## Say it at work

- Let's review our capacity for the upcoming sprint before we pull in any more backlog items.
- Please adjust the sprint scope to match our reduced capacity during the holiday week.
