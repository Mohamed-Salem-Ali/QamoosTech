## Definition

The planned sequence and dates for the work on a project, from start to delivery, including the main milestones.

## Where you hear it

In project plans, client updates, and questions such as "What is the timeline for this feature?"

## Examples

- The timeline assumes the design is approved by Monday.
- We moved the timeline by two weeks after the vendor delay.
- The timeline shows the design in week two and the launch in week six.

## Common mistake

Promising a date before the work is estimated. A timeline without estimates is a guess.

## Don't confuse with

A deadline is one fixed date. A timeline is the full plan that leads to it.

## Say it at work

- Let me check the timeline with the team before I commit to a date.
