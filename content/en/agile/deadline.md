## Definition

The date or time by which something must be finished.

## Where you hear it

Projects and client communication.

## Examples

- The deadline for the first version is Friday.
- We might miss the deadline, so let me explain why.

## Common mistake

Telling the client about a delay only at the last minute. Warn them as soon as you see the risk.
