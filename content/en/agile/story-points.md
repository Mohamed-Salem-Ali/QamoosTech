## Definition

Story points are a unit of measure used in Agile to estimate the overall effort required to complete a user story. They represent a relative scale considering complexity, risk, and the amount of work involved rather than absolute time.

## Where you hear it

During sprint planning meetings, backlog grooming sessions, or when discussing team velocity.

## Examples

- We assigned five story points to this task because it involves complex database migrations.
- The team decided that this user story is too large and needs to be broken down into smaller story points.
- Three story points for the login form and eight for the whole checkout flow.

## Common mistake

Treating story points as direct equivalents to hours or days, which undermines their purpose as a relative estimation tool.

## Don't confuse with

Story points measure relative effort and complexity, whereas hours estimate absolute time.

## Say it at work

- Let's use Fibonacci numbers to assign story points for this backlog item.
- Could you please update the story points on this ticket before the planning meeting?
