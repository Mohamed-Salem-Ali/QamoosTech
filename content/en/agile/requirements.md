## Definition

The things a product or feature must do, and the conditions it must meet, written so the team and the client agree on what to build.

## Where you hear it

In kickoff meetings, specification documents, and requests from product owners and clients.

## Examples

- The requirements say the report must export to PDF.
- We need the requirements signed off before development starts.
- The requirements say the invoice must show the VAT number and the due date.

## Common mistake

Writing a requirement as a solution, such as "use Redis", instead of the need, such as "answer in under 200 ms".

## Don't confuse with

Requirements say what is needed. A specification describes in detail how the system will do it.

## Say it at work

- Can you send me the requirements for the export feature?
- The requirements changed again, so the estimate is off.
