## Definition

The ordered list of all work the team may do in the future: features, bugs, and improvements.

## Where you hear it

Planning meetings and project tools like Jira.

## Examples

- Add this idea to the backlog.
- The product owner prioritizes the backlog every week.

## Common mistake

Letting the backlog grow forever. Review it and remove tasks nobody will ever do.
