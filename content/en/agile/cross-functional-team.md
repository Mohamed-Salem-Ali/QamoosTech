## Definition

A team with all the skills needed to finish the work, for example developers, designers, and testers together.

## Where you hear it

Agile organizations and interviews.

## Examples

- I work in a cross-functional team with designers and QA.
- A cross-functional team can ship without waiting for other departments.

## Common mistake

Using the term when you only talked to another team once. It means working together on the same goal.
