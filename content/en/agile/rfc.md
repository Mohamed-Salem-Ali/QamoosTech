## Definition

A proposal document shared with the team to gather comments before a decision is made. It explains the problem, the options, and a recommendation.

## Where you hear it

In engineering teams and open-source projects, when a change affects many people.

## Examples

- I posted an RFC about moving the API to GraphQL.
- Please add your comments to the RFC by Friday.
- The RFC for the new logging library got comments from three teams.

## Common mistake

Treating an RFC as a formality and deciding before the comments arrive.

## Don't confuse with

An RFC asks for comments before a decision. An architecture decision record (ADR) records the decision after it is made.
