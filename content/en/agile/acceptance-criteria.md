## Definition

The specific conditions and requirements that a feature must meet before the client or product owner approves it.

## Where you hear it

In sprint planning, requirement gathering meetings, and ticket descriptions.

## Examples

- The login button must be disabled until both email and password fields are filled.
- The exported report must download as a CSV file within three seconds.
- The upload page shows an error message when the file is larger than 10 MB.

## Common mistake

Writing vague requirements like "make it look nice" instead of testable, concrete conditions.

## Don't confuse with

Acceptance criteria define specific conditions for a single user story, whereas definition of done sets the quality standards for all stories in a sprint.

## Say it at work

- Before we mark this ticket as ready, we need to finalize the acceptance criteria with the product owner.
- Please review the acceptance criteria on this task to ensure all edge cases are covered.
