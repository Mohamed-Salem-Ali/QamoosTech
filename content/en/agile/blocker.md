## Definition

Anything that stops you from continuing your work, such as missing access or waiting for another team.

## Where you hear it

Daily standups and status updates.

## Examples

- My blocker is that I still do not have access to the staging server.
- I am blocked until the API is ready.

## Common mistake

Waiting silently for hours. Raise a blocker as soon as you see it.

## Don't confuse with

Blocker vs. Bottleneck: A blocker is a specific issue preventing an individual from completing a task, whereas a bottleneck is a point in a process where the flow is restricted due to limited capacity.

## Say it at work

- Hey team, I've hit a blocker with the database permissions, so I might need some help to move forward.
- Please note that the integration task is currently marked as a blocker for the upcoming release.
