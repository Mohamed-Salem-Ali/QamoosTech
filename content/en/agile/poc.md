## Definition

A PoC is a small, focused project or exercise designed to verify that a specific idea, technology, or technical approach is feasible before committing full resources. It is not intended to be a production-ready product, but rather a way to validate assumptions.

## Where you hear it

During technical planning meetings, project kickoff phases, or when evaluating new third-party tools.

## Examples

- We need to build a quick PoC to see if this library can handle our data volume.
- The team spent two days on a PoC to validate the new authentication flow.

## Common mistake

Confusing a PoC with an MVP; a PoC is a throwaway experiment to test feasibility, while an MVP is a functional product intended for real users.
