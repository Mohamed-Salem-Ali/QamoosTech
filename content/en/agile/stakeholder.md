## Definition

Anyone who is affected by the project or cares about its result: clients, users, managers, other teams.

## Where you hear it

Meetings, requirements, and project reports.

## Examples

- We presented the demo to the stakeholders.
- Who are the stakeholders for this feature?

## Common mistake

Thinking a stakeholder is just your manager. Users, support, and other teams are stakeholders too.
