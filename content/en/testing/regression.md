## Definition

A bug where something that used to work stops working after a change.

## Where you hear it

Release testing and bug reports.

## Examples

- This is a regression: the export worked last week.
- We added a test to prevent this regression from coming back.

## Common mistake

Fixing a regression without adding a test. It may come back again.
