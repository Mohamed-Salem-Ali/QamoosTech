## Definition

Making websites usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboards only, or have low vision. "a11y" is a short form: "a", 11 letters, "y".

## Where you hear it

Audits (Lighthouse), legal requirements, and design reviews.

## Examples

- Add an `alt` text to every image for accessibility.
- Can you reach every button using only the keyboard?

## Common mistake

Adding accessibility at the end. It is much cheaper to build with semantic HTML from the start.
