## Definition

A reusable piece of user interface, such as a button, a card, or a whole page section, built with its own code and style.

## Where you hear it

React, Vue, Angular, and design systems.

## Examples

- Let's turn the product card into a reusable component.
- This component is too big. Split it into smaller ones.

## Common mistake

Building huge components that do everything. Small focused components are easier to test and reuse.
