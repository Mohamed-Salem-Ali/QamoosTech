## Definition

Building a page so its layout adapts to any screen size, from a small phone to a large desktop monitor.

## Where you hear it

Frontend tasks, design reviews, and client feedback ("it looks broken on my phone").

## Examples

- The page is fully responsive, so it works on phones and tablets.
- We design mobile-first and add layout for larger screens later.

## Common mistake

Testing only on your own screen. Check at least a narrow phone width and a wide desktop width.
